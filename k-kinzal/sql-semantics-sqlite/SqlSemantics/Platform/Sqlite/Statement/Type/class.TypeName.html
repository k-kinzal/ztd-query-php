A type name as SQLite reads it: one or more words and up to two signed numbers in parentheses.

Rule: SQLITE-TYPE-NAME-001. SQLite does not look the name up. It records the words as written, and when the text starts with a quoted word it keeps only what that first quoted word contains. It derives a type affinity from the recorded text by the five ordered substring rules of the manual and ignores the numbers. The words therefore keep how they are quoted. Source: https://sqlite.org/datatype3.html#determination_of_column_affinity. Status: Implemented.
