An ON DELETE, ON UPDATE or ON INSERT clause of a foreign key.

Rule: SQLITE-FK-ACTION-001. The reaction applies to the child rows when the parent key is deleted or updated. An ON INSERT clause is grammatical and has no effect. Source: https://sqlite.org/foreignkeys.html#fk_actions. Status: Implemented.
