The operator between two terms of a FROM clause: a comma, JOIN, or JOIN after up to three words.

Rule: SQLITE-JOIN-OPERATOR-001. The grammar admits one join keyword and then up to two arbitrary names before JOIN; SQLite accepts the words only when each is a join keyword and the combination is meaningful. A word that is no bare join keyword is kept as the name written. NATURAL merges the common columns; LEFT, RIGHT and FULL select the sides whose rows are extended with NULLs (LEFT and RIGHT together are FULL); INNER or CROSS with an outer keyword, OUTER alone, and any other word are an unknown join type, which SQLite reports and treats as an inner join. Source: https://sqlite.org/lang_select.html#the_from_clause. Status: Implemented.
