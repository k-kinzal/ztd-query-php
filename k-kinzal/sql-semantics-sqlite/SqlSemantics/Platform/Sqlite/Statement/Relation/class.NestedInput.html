Public API: explicitly declared with @visibility public.

A FROM clause term list written in parentheses and used as one term.

Rule: SQLITE-NESTED-INPUT-001. The terms inside are joined first. Their relations stay visible under their own names; a correlation name on the parentheses names the joined result as well. Around a single term the parentheses and their correlation name replace the correlation name of that term. Source: https://sqlite.org/lang_select.html#the_from_clause. Status: Implemented.
