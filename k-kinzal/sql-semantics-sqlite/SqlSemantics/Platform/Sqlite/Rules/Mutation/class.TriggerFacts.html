Derives the facts of a trigger definition.

Rule: SQLITE-TRIGGER-SCOPE-001. The watched table is resolved once. The WHEN condition and every statement of the program see, under the qualifiers NEW and OLD only, a row of the watched table with its implicit columns: NEW in INSERT and UPDATE triggers, OLD in UPDATE and DELETE triggers. A column of UPDATE OF that the table certainly lacks is reported. A BEFORE or AFTER trigger, BEFORE being the default, on a view and an INSTEAD OF trigger on a table are reported (SQLITE-RELATION-KIND-001). In the program, a written table qualified with a schema, an index choice and a bind parameter are reported, as SQLite rejects them (SQLITE-PROGRAM-ONLY-001). Terminates: one pass over the program. Source: https://sqlite.org/lang_createtrigger.html. Status: Implemented.
