Public API: explicitly declared with @visibility public.

An INSERT of rows written in VALUES.

Mirrors PostgreSQL's InsertStmt whose selectStmt is a bare VALUES list: each value is assigned to its column on its own, and may be DEFAULT. The facts follow PG-INSERT-001. Source: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-insert.html. Status: Implemented.
