final class Alternative
Public API: explicitly declared with @visibility public.

One right-hand side of a rule, as the sequence of items written between | separators.

Symbols, actions, predicates and modifiers are kept in the order they appear, so an action followed by more symbols is where Bison would put a mid-rule action.
