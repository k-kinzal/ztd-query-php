The entry rules of the expression family: the methods other families and the statement dispatcher call.

Rule: MYSQL-EXPRESSION-ENTRY-001. Scope: expr, bool_pri, predicate, bit_expr, simple_expr, operators, CASE, casts, intervals, row constructors, subquery expressions and MATCH. A method delegates to the rule class of the level it lowers: MYSQL-CONDITION-001, MYSQL-PREDICATE-001, MYSQL-BIT-EXPR-001, MYSQL-SIMPLE-EXPR-001, MYSQL-EXPRESSION-LIST-001 and MYSQL-INTERVAL-UNIT-001. Source: https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/expressions.html. Status: Implemented.
