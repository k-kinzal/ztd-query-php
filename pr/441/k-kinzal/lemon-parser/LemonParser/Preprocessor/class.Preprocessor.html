Public API: explicitly declared with @visibility public.

Settles %if, %ifdef, %ifndef, %else and %endif before the file is scanned.

As in Lemon's preprocess_input, a directive counts only at the start of a line, the directive lines and the excluded lines are blanked rather than removed, so every position in the result is a position in the original, and an excluded region may nest further directives.
