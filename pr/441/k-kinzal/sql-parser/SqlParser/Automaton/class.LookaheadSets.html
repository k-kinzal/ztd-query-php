The LALR(1) lookahead of every reduction, by the method of DeRemer and Pennello.

Each nonterminal transition of the automaton is a node. Its direct reads are the terminals that can follow it at once; the reads relation adds what follows a nullable nonterminal after it; the includes relation adds what follows a rule that ends with it. The lookahead of a completed rule is the union over the transitions the rule was entered from.
