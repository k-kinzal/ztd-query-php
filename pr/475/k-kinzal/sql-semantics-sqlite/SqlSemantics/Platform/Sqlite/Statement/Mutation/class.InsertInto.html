The head of an INSERT statement: the verb, the conflict resolution, the written table and the column list.

REPLACE is the short spelling of INSERT OR REPLACE; the model keeps which one is written. Source: https://sqlite.org/lang_insert.html, https://sqlite.org/lang_replace.html.
