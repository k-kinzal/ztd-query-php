The entry point of the access family: roles, privileges, role membership, default privileges and owned objects.

Rule: PG-ACCESS-001. Scope: the statement nonterminals of the family (CreateRoleStmt, CreateUserStmt, CreateGroupStmt, AlterRoleStmt, AlterRoleSetStmt, AlterGroupStmt, DropRoleStmt, GrantStmt, RevokeStmt, GrantRoleStmt, RevokeRoleStmt, AlterDefaultPrivilegesStmt, DropOwnedStmt, ReassignOwnedStmt), each handed to the rule of its group. Termination: each statement is lowered by one rule; lists are flattened iteratively. Source: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-commands.html. Status: Implemented.
