The entry point of the utility family: transactions, configuration, EXPLAIN, maintenance, locking, notification, CALL and DO.

Rule: PG-UTILITY-001. Scope: the statement nonterminals of the family (php .agent/pg-families.php Utility), each handed to the rule of its group, and SetResetClause, FunctionSetResetClause. Termination: each statement is lowered by one rule; lists are flattened iteratively. Source: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-commands.html. Status: Implemented.
