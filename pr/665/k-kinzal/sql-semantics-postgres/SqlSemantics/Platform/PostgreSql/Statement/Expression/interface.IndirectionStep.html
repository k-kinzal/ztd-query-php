interface IndirectionStep
    extends Clause
Public API: explicitly declared with @visibility public.

One step applied to a value or an assignment target: a field selection, a subscript, a slice or a star.

Mirrors the items of PostgreSQL's A_Indirection node. Steps follow an expression in parentheses or a parameter, and the column of an INSERT or UPDATE target. Source: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-expressions.html#SQL-EXPRESSIONS-SUBSCRIPTS, https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-expressions.html#FIELD-SELECTION.
