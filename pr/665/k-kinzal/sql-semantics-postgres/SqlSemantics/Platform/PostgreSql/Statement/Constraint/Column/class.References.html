A REFERENCES column constraint: a foreign key of one column.

Mirrors CONSTR_FOREIGN with pktable, pk_attrs, fk_matchtype and the actions. The referenced table is resolved (PG-FOREIGN-KEY-001); its facts are the relation facts of this constraint. Source: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-createtable.html#SQL-CREATETABLE-PARMS-REFERENCES.
