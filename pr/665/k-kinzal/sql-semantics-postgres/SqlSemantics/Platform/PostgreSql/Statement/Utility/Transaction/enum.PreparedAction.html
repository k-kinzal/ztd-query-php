The steps of a two-phase commit.

Mirrors TRANS_STMT_PREPARE, TRANS_STMT_COMMIT_PREPARED and TRANS_STMT_ROLLBACK_PREPARED of PostgreSQL's TransactionStmtKind. The value is the keyword spelling.
