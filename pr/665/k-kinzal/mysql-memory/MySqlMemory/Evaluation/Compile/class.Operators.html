public function negated(Scalar $operand, Scope $scope, Scalar $node): Evaluable
source§

Compiles the negation of an operand as the server rewrites it before it evaluates anything.

The negation of a test of NULL is the opposite test; of AND or OR, the other operator over the negated operands; of XOR, XOR with its left operand negated; and of a negation of a test or of a logical operator, that test or operator itself. A test of NULL a negation makes, as opposed to one written, is evaluated for every row even when its operand is constant. Source: https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/logical-operators.html.
