enum NameQuote
Public API: explicitly declared with @visibility public.

The delimiter or unquoted keyword form of a name, independent of its decoded value.

Keyword keeps a bare keyword distinct from an ordinary bare identifier. Its lexical boundary can matter before parentheses or a qualification dot. No token, parser rule or original text fragment is retained.
