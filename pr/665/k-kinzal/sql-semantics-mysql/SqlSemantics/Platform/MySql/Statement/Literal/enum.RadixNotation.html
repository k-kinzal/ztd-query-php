enum RadixNotation
Public API: explicitly declared with @visibility public.

The quoted or numeric-prefix notation of a hexadecimal or bit literal.

Quoted hexadecimal literals require pairs of digits; numeric prefixes require at least one digit. The quoted prefix may use either letter case, whereas the numeric prefixes are always 0x and 0b. Source: https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/hexadecimal-literals.html, https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/bit-value-literals.html.
