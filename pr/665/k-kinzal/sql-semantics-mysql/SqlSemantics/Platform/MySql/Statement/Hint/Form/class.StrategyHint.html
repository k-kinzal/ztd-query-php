A subquery hint: an optional query block and the strategies it enables or disables.

SEMIJOIN and NO_SEMIJOIN take any of DUPSWEEDOUT, FIRSTMATCH, LOOSESCAN and MATERIALIZATION, or none; SUBQUERY takes exactly one of INTOEXISTS and MATERIALIZATION. Strategy names are keywords, kept in upper case. Source: https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/optimizer-hints.html#optimizer-hints-subquery.
