The binary logical operators of MySQL.

&& is a synonym of AND and, unless PIPES_AS_CONCAT is set, || is a synonym of OR; both are written in their keyword form. Each case holds the keyword. Source: https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/logical-operators.html.
