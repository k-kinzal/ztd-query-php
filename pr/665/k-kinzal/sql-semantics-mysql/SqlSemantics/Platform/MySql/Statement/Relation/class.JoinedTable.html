A join of two table references with its ON condition or USING column list.

The structure follows the parse of the release, where a join can nest on the right without parentheses (t JOIN u JOIN v ON c joins t to the join of u and v in 8.0 and later). A natural join takes no condition and its right operand is no unparenthesized join; LEFT and RIGHT joins require a condition; a comma list as an operand is written in parentheses.

Rule: MYSQL-JOIN-001. The operands and the condition are derived by MYSQL-FROM-SCOPE-001 and combined by MYSQL-JOIN-COLUMNS-001. Source: https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/join.html. Status: Implemented.
