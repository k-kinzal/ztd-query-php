The runs of statements a branching statement can take.

An if, a switch and a try each run one of several lists of statements. Each list is one arm; an arm that cannot fall through to the next statement is left out, since it cannot be the one taken on the way to anything after it.

The runs of statements a branching statement may have taken, or null when it does not branch.

An arm that cannot finish — it returns, throws, breaks or continues — cannot have been the one taken on the way to a statement after it, so only the arms that fall through are kept.
