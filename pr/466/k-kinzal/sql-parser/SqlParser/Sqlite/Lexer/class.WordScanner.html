Reads SQLite's identifiers and keywords, deciding the three keywords that depend on context.

WINDOW, OVER and FILTER cannot fall back to identifiers in the grammar, so the tokenizer decides for them by looking around: WINDOW is a keyword before a name and AS, OVER after a closing parenthesis and before a parenthesis or a name, FILTER after a closing parenthesis and before an opening one.
