The words and operators MySQL's lexer turns into keyword tokens.

Function names are keywords only when a parenthesis follows them at once, which is what lets a column be called count. Two lookups depend on the SQL mode: NOT binds tightly under HIGH_NOT_PRECEDENCE, and || is OR unless PIPES_AS_CONCAT makes it concatenation.
