The entry point of the manipulation family: INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE, MERGE, COPY, prepared statements and cursors.

Rule: PG-MANIPULATION-001. Scope: PreparableStmt and the statements of the family, each lowered by the rule of its nonterminal (PG-INSERT-LOWER-001, PG-CHANGE-LOWER-001, PG-MERGE-LOWER-001, PG-COPY-LOWER-001, PG-PREPARED-LOWER-001, PG-CURSOR-LOWER-001). Source: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-commands.html. Status: Implemented.
