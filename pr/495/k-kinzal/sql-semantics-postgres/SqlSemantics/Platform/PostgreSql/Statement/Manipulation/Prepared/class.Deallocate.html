DEALLOCATE: removal of one prepared statement, or of all of them.

Mirrors PostgreSQL's DeallocateStmt, whose name is NULL for ALL. The keyword PREPARE after DEALLOCATE is ignored. Rule: PG-DEALLOCATE-001: the prepared statements are session state; nothing is derived. Source: https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/sql-deallocate.html. Status: Implemented.
