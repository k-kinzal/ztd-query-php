Reads MySQL's numeric literals and the identifiers that begin with a digit.

An integer becomes the narrowest of NUM, LONG_NUM and ULONGLONG_NUM that holds it, and DECIMAL_NUM beyond that. Digits followed by letters are an identifier, unless the letter is an exponent.
