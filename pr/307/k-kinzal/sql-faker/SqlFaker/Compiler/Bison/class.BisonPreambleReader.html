Reads the declarations section of a Bison grammar.

The section ends at the %% that opens the rules, and that separator is consumed here so the rules reader starts on a rule. A declaration whose arguments do not match its directive is dropped rather than aborting the section: MySQL's grammar carries directives this parser has no model for, and losing the declarations after one of them would cost more than the one.
