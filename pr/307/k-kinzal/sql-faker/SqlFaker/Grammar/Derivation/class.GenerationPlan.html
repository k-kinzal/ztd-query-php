Directs one act of generation: where to start, which productions to take, and how deep to go.

A generator walks a grammar that describes far more SQL than any single caller wants, so the plan is what narrows it. Every plan is immutable and every refinement answers a new one, which is what lets one plan be reused across generations without a previous walk leaking into the next.
