One thing that has to happen, on the way to the call, for its argument to have the value it has.

A step is the node that does it: an assignment, a global or static declaration, an unset, the start of one pass through a foreach, or a closure parameter taking whatever a caller passes. A step can instead hold alternatives — whole runs of steps, any one of which may have happened — when the paths had to be gathered up to keep their number bounded. Entering a closure is a step as well: the names it lists are the ones the closure defines for itself, as parameters or not at all, rather than taking them from the body it is written in.
