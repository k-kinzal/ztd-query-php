final class ResourceLoader
Public API: explicitly declared with @visibility public.

Reads source documents from HTTP(S) URLs or local paths, with pinned snapshots.

Reads are limited to 16 MiB and remembered per URI, failures included. A missing local snapshot is fetched from the source URI, verified against the digest and cached; a document whose digest differs is rejected.
