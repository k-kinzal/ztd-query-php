8 * The built-in functions whose name is a keyword of the grammar and whose arguments are a plain list of expressions.
9 *
10 * Each case holds the keyword the function is written with. The grammar
11 * names them in function_call_keyword, function_call_nonkeyword,
12 * function_call_conflict, geometry_function and grouping_operation. SUBSTR
13 * and MID are the keyword SUBSTRING, SCHEMA() is DATABASE(), SESSION_USER()
14 * and SYSTEM_USER() are USER() to the lexer; ADDDATE and SUBDATE here are
15 * the forms with a number of days.

17 * https://dev.mysql.com/doc/refman/8.4/en/function-resolution.html.
18 *
19 * @visibility public
